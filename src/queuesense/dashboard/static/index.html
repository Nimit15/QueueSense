How QueueSense Works in 4 Steps

Every decision is observable, auditable, and designed for mixed Indian traffic where lanes don't exist and vehicle sizes vary wildly.

01

Dynamic Density Green Extension

AI detects real-time vehicle queue (PCU-weighted: cars=1.0, buses=2.2, autos=1.0, bikes=0.5, trucks=2.0) and arrival flow. When pressure exceeds threshold on one approach, green time extends automatically — pulsing badge shows AI Extended Green +10s — Clearing Heavy West Flow. Phase ends early the moment queue clears.

02

Downstream Queue Spillback Prevention

Connected downstream junction (J2) and receiving link (J1→J2) are monitored continuously. When exit road occupancy reaches ≥30%, the downstream link highlights Exit Road Filling Up — Stopping Upstream Green Beforehand and QueueSense holds upstream traffic safely behind the stop line — zero cars trapped in the intersection box — while switching green to the unblocked cross-street.

03

Downstream-Flush Emergency Green Corridor

When an ambulance spawns (randomly or via button), flashing red/blue lights appear. Downstream junction J2 turns green FIRST to flush blocking cars out of the way, then J1 opens a smooth Green Corridor wave. Result: 28.5s ambulance travel time vs 52.0s naive preemption vs 76.0s no preemption — verified across 300 SUMO runs.

04

3-State Fault-Aware Safety Supervisor

Every decision passes through a hard safety gate: CLEAR (proceed normally), ELEVATED_RISK (shorten green, prepare clearance), BLOCKED (suppress phase, hold red). On sensor fault or dropout, a 3-strike watchdog triggers immediate fallback to C-DAC Fixed-Time plan — identical safety rules (min 10s green, max 60s, yellow/all-red clearance) enforced across ALL 4 benchmarked controllers.
